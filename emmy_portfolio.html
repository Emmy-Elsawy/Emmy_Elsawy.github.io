Research & Publications
Under Review · 2026

Image Forgery Localization via Multi-Channel U-Net Architecture

El-Essawi A., El-Sawy E., Nasser A., Hegazy N., Anwar M. — Dept. of Artificial Intelligence, MUST University, Giza, Egypt

Proposes a pixel-level forgery localization framework fusing RGB with three forensic feature maps (ELA, noise residuals, Canny edges) into a 6-channel U-Net input. Trained on CASIA ITDE v2.0 with a hybrid Focal-BCE + Dice loss; achieves Dice 0.4004 and 46.43% forged pixel recall, reducing the generalization gap from ~41% to ~18% compared to the baseline model.
